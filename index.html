Questions

Where is SIMOO located?

On Mamrya Road, Dire Dawa 3000, Ethiopia. Tap “Get Directions” above to open the map.

Specialty coffees, tea, cakes, small plates and quick bites.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking is available.

Of course. Many guests come to work remotely, study, or simply relax.
